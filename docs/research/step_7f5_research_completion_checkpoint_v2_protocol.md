# Step 7F.5 Research Completion Checkpoint V2 Protocol

Status: static artifact integration. No CKKS execution, policy modification,
claim promotion, or manuscript writing is allowed.

## Purpose

Checkpoint V2 extends, but never modifies, the immutable V1 checkpoint. It
binds the finite-domain decision-contract activation control to the prior 16
authoritative evidence packs and records the exact boundary between:

- the natural-data candidate-synthesis effect, which remains `BLOCKED`; and
- the finite-domain activation mechanism, which is `SUPPORTED`.

The synthetic development control cannot promote or reinterpret the natural
development, primary confirmatory, or independent-training-seed populations.

## Lineage

The verifier:

1. verifies checkpoint V1 through its complete tree and historical Git source
   bindings;
2. verifies all 17 current authoritative evidence packs;
3. binds V1 as an immutable predecessor by manifest and complete-tree digest;
4. refuses to overwrite either checkpoint version.

When a bound source document advances after a checkpoint, the verifier checks
the blob at that checkpoint's freezer commit. This keeps historical snapshots
verifiable without requiring mutable document paths to remain byte-identical.

## Activation Gates

The activation pack must retain:

- four selection arms and four no-retuning locked audits;
- 14 selection trials, 10 repairs, and 42 selection key runs;
- 1,344 selection and 384 audit encrypted sample evaluations;
- four audit PASS results with zero flips, violations, or retuning;
- Direct Policy V2 and Security Policy V2 byte identities;
- two pre-encryption implementation-recovery histories with zero encrypted
  key runs;
- `paper_claim_allowed=false`.

The narrow-margin full arm must differ from its graph-fixed arm, while the
wide-margin arms retain the same initial scale. This is a finite-domain
mechanism check, not evidence that natural margins generally dominate
configuration selection.

## Evidence Hygiene

Complete-tree digests include every file under each pack. Generated Python
artifacts (`__pycache__`, `.pyc`, and `.pyo`) are prohibited so that local
compilation cannot silently change a frozen pack's tree identity.

## Remaining Gates

Checkpoint V2 remains a `POST_CONFIRMATORY_RESEARCH_CHECKPOINT` with
`paper_claim_allowed=false`. Exact lattice-estimator execution, release
tagging, external archival, independent-machine replay, and explicit paper
claim admission remain separate gates.
