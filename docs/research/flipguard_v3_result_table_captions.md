# FlipGuard V3 Result-Table Captions

Status: publication-input specification only.

1. **Claim and evidence registry.** Only rows with
   `paper_admitted=true` authorize paper claims; blocked claims remain visible.
2. **Workload/model/input scope.** Declared graph adapters, scalar-replicated
   packing, deterministic repeated partitions, and independent training-seed
   extension.
3. **Primary direct selection and locked audit.** Seed 0 development is
   separated from seeds 1-4 confirmatory evidence.
4. **Formal trial reduction.** Direct 70/700 overall and 56/560 confirmatory;
   1,100 is historical pre-security execution accounting only.
5. **Paired confirmatory latency.** Inference uses 10 dataset-model clusters,
   not 1,800 independent raw pairs.
6. **Direct-synthesis ablation.** Scoped empirical repair benefit; no universal
   repair-success claim.
7. **NO_SAFE controls.** Budgeted and finite-domain abstention do not prove
   global infeasibility.
8. **Policy sensitivity.** Tested-grid invariance and margin-floor coverage do
   not establish optimality.
9. **Structural polynomial holdout.** Report 25/25 selection, 24 audit PASS,
   and one reserve-policy REJECT without a decision flip.
10. **Scoped non-tabular extension.** Sobel, Harris, and CNN-lite results are
    limited to declared finite inputs and scalar-replicated execution.
11. **Independent training-seed extension.** Nine trained models across three
    datasets and three predeclared seeds; not universal model generalization.
12. **Security attestation.** Q and QP admission and estimator-model
    sensitivity; not exact runtime-distribution equivalence.
13. **Failure taxonomy.** Preserve REJECTED, FAILED, NO_SAFE, audit rejection,
    and representation-layer identity failures.

Provider and EVA evidence belongs to an appendix-only table.
