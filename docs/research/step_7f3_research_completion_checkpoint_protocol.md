# Step 7F.3 Research Completion Checkpoint Protocol

Status: static artifact integration. No CKKS execution, policy modification,
claim promotion, or manuscript writing is allowed.

## Purpose

The checkpoint binds the authoritative post-confirmatory evidence without
copying, replacing, or reinterpreting a frozen pack. It answers a narrower
question than paper admission:

> Are the declared claim states, policy identities, population accounting,
> scientific negative results, and evidence-pack bytes mutually consistent?

## Required Bindings

The index covers:

- primary final confirmatory evidence;
- Security V2 attestation and bounded catalog comparison;
- validation identity V2;
- confirmatory NO_SAFE controls;
- structural selection, locked audit, and failure analysis;
- paired latency and policy sensitivity;
- direct-synthesis ablation;
- independent training/data-split seed extension;
- BSDS500 Sobel and Harris holdouts;
- scalar-replicated MNIST CNN-lite holdout;
- observed tabular certification;
- conditional analytical readiness V2.

Each pack is bound by its manifest digest and complete tree digest. An
internal `SHA256SUMS` is verified when present; older packs without one remain
bound by their complete tree digest.

## Semantic Gates

The verifier fails closed unless:

- Direct Policy V2 and Security Policy V2 digests match;
- formal catalog accounting is 1,100 raw, 700 admitted, and 400 excluded;
- formal trial reduction is computed against 700 and 560;
- the structural result remains 25 selected, 24 audit PASS, and one disclosed
  numerical REJECT with no retuning;
- independent training-seed evidence contains nine instances;
- Sobel, Harris, and CNN-lite retain their frozen policy identities;
- the natural-data decision-contract synthesis effect remains `BLOCKED`;
- analytical propagation is `SUPPORTED` but instantiated CKKS analytical
  certification remains `BLOCKED` with 0/50 proof-eligible candidates;
- validation identity remains CLASS A for 50/50 instances with no rerun.

## Claim Boundary

This is a `POST_CONFIRMATORY_RESEARCH_CHECKPOINT`, not a final paper artifact.
It preserves the closed claim-state vocabulary and forces
`paper_claim_allowed=false`.

Remaining manual gates are release tagging, external archival,
independent-machine deterministic replay, and explicit paper claim admission.
