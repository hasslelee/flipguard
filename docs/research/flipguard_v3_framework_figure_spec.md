validation, failure-aware repair, certify/reject/NO_SAFE, literal lock, and
disjoint no-retuning audit. Evaluation-only side path: the Security-V2
compliant bounded catalog. External providers are appendix inputs to the same
gate, not part of the core path.

## Figure 3: Margin theorem versus utilization policy

Show `e<m` as the sufficient decision-preservation condition and
`e<rho*m`, `rho=0.5`, as a stricter predeclared operational policy. Label
`1-rho` as reserved margin. Do not label `rho` a theorem constant.
