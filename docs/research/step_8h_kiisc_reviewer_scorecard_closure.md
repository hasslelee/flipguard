## Reviewer attack checklist

1. **“S29 is faster merely because its scale is lower.”** The measured S32/S29
   interval crosses one, so the manuscript claims a literal effect and explicitly
   rejects a latency-superiority effect.
2. **“LeNet has no baseline.”** The manuscript reports a frozen-catalog coverage
   failure, not a direct-versus-catalog performance victory.
3. **“Security was falsified.”** The original global status was scoped to a
   different input set and an already inadmissible profile; candidate-specific
   replay passes two declared classical adapters. The Xe and quantum caveats
   remain.
4. **“Fifty rows inflate the sample size.”** The primary inference unit is ten
   dataset-model clusters with repeated partitions; the manuscript never calls
   them 50 independent workloads.
5. **“The certificate is a proof of future behavior.”** SAFE is finite-artifact
   admission. The preserved structural policy rejection demonstrates why
   locked audit and scoped claims are necessary.
