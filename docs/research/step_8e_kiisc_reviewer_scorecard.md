# Step 8e: KIISC Reviewer Scorecard

## Reviewer concern

The research needs a reviewer-facing action sheet, not only a self-assigned
score. Each point must state what evidence earns it, which comparison class is
relevant, how a skeptical reviewer can attack it, and what response is
required before the journal overlay is admitted.

## Literature precedent

Focused JKIISC primitive papers reward a compact mathematical claim plus
implementation evidence. CISC-S 2025 MultiMax metadata shows that a current
CKKS primitive comparison can be recognized without claiming framework-scale
breadth. HECATE/ELASM represent compiler-framework expectations: standard ML
graphs, explicit search spaces, and measured error/latency. Application-Aware
Approximate HE represents formal-scope expectations. FlipGuard should combine
its strongest systems properties with one exact multiclass proposition and
two standard architectures, not imitate every neighboring paper.

## Falsification test

Scores are recomputed from actual evidence. `PLAN_UNSUPPORTED`, an
inadmissible candidate, model accuracy that invalidates the benchmark, a
locked-audit rejection, or no observed gap activation must appear in the
scorecard and lower the corresponding target. No score is allowed to imply an
acceptance or award probability.
