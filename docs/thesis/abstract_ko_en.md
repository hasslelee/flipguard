# English Abstract

## FlipGuard: Decision-Integrity-Aware Direct Synthesis and Validation of CKKS Configurations

The CKKS approximate homomorphic-encryption scheme enables computation over encrypted real-valued data, but its numerical error and latency depend strongly on the scale, modulus chain, polynomial degree, and execution path. In applications that compare a score with a threshold, even a small approximation error can change the final decision. Execution success or an aggregate error metric alone is therefore insufficient for admitting a configuration. Existing compilers and autotuners have advanced parameter, scale, bootstrapping, latency, and accuracy optimization, while a common threshold decision-integrity admission rule and a no-retuning audit remain distinct concerns across candidate sources.

This thesis presents FlipGuard, which directly synthesizes exact CKKS literals from supported computation graphs and threshold decision-integrity contracts. FlipGuard derives the scale, Q/P chain, and LogN from graph facts and validates each candidate with at most {{N:max_encrypted_trials}} encrypted trials. It applies bounded repairs only to predeclared numerical or level failures and stops at the first SAFE candidate. When no SAFE candidate can be established, it abstains with NO_SAFE. The selected literal is then replayed without retuning on a locked audit disjoint from configuration validation. The sufficient decision-preservation condition, `e_c(x)<m(x)`, is explicitly separated from the operational reserve policy, `e_c(x)<{{N:primary_alpha}}m(x)`; {{N:margin_utilization_cap}} is a predeclared margin-utilization cap rather than a theoretically optimal constant.

The primary evaluation uses {{N:combined_descriptive_instances}} workload-partition instances formed from {{N:primary_datasets}} datasets, {{N:primary_model_graphs}} model graphs, and {{N:deterministic_partitions}} deterministic repeated partitions. Seed 0 is development/descriptive, whereas seeds 1--4 are post-freeze confirmatory. The Security-V2-admitted formal bounded catalog contains {{N:formal_catalog_all}} candidates overall and {{N:formal_catalog_confirmatory}} confirmatory candidates. Direct synthesis used {{N:direct_trials_all}} and {{N:direct_trials_confirmatory}} candidate trials, respectively, yielding a {{N:formal_trial_reduction_all|.0%}} formal reduction in both populations. No-retuning locked audit passed for {{N:confirmatory_locked_audit_pass}}/{{N:confirmatory_instances}} confirmatory and {{N:development_locked_audit_pass}}/{{N:development_instances}} development instances with {{N:primary_locked_audit_retuning}} retuning. A predeclared budget control returned NO_SAFE in {{N:no_safe_budget}}/{{N:no_safe_budget_total}} instances, and a finite-domain control returned NO_SAFE in {{N:no_safe_finite_domain}}/{{N:no_safe_finite_domain_total}} instances.

Under a paired protocol on one host, the confirmatory dataset-model-cluster geometric mean of Security-V2 bounded-catalog total latency divided by direct total latency was {{N:paired_total_ratio_confirmatory|.6f}}, with a cluster-bootstrap 95% confidence interval of [{{N:paired_total_ci_low|.6f}}, {{N:paired_total_ci_high|.6f}}]. For the deeper polynomial `mlp_square_poly3` graph, all {{N:structural_selected}} instances were selected; locked audit produced {{N:structural_audit_pass}} PASS outcomes and {{N:structural_reserve_reject}} reserve-policy REJECT without a decision flip. Sobel, Harris, scalar-replicated CNN-lite, and a {{N:independent_training_datasets}}-dataset by {{N:independent_training_seeds_per_dataset}}-independent-training/data-seed extension provide evidence only within their declared finite scopes.
